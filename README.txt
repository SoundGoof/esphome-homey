With ESPHome you can easily build your own smart devices based on ESP. The ESPHome app for Homey connects them to Homey over a direct local connection.

Support for ESPHome devices with Light, Switch, Sensor, Binary sensor, Cover, Climate, Fan, Lock, Button, Number, Select, Media player and more entities. The ESPHome app for Homey lets you control these entities via Homey and in Flows, and gather their data in Insights.

For this integration Homey connects directly to your ESPHome devices on the local network via the Native API, with automatic discovery over mDNS.
